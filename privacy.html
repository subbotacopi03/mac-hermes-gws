Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026.

Mac Hermes GWS is used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's Google account data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Sheets) solely to perform the owner's own automations on the owner's computer.

  • Data is processed only on the owner's computer and is not sold, shared with or transferred to third parties.
  • No data of other people's Google accounts is accessed.
  • Google user data is used only to provide the automation features and in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
